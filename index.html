Things I made

Three things I built because I wanted them to exist.

Studytown

A small town at dusk, in the browser, where friends study together. People near you can hear you, and voices get louder as you walk closer. Put on your headphones and the town goes quiet.

It has no image or audio files. The city, the characters, the music and the weather are all generated in code.

Hyperfocus

A task app for iPhone, made for ADHD brains. It has one goal, to take the friction out of starting. I made it for people who stall where I stall.

You race your own recent days, and the target is a typical day, so you pass a few of them almost every time. A missed day breaks nothing.

The code is on GitHub

Stillleaf

A reading app for the Mac. It keeps track of what you read, and a garden made of small characters grows across the window as you get closer to the day's goal.

Hêrte

A philosophical fantasy epic in five acts and an encore. I have been building it since March 2025, and I expect it to take decades.

Sattāvarta, the Spiral of Being.

Why go on when there is no reason to?

A creator god named Ishar'el demands that existence justify itself. He is tragic, and he is right to ask. Four people answer him, and the book argues all four answers at full strength.

  • Kavi

    We are alive, and thus we go on.

    The narrator. Acceptance without surrender.

  • Ariṣṭa

    I will hold you. Where you go, I will go; where you stay, I will stay.

    Love as sustenance.

  • Levitas

    This is what it costs to make a god hesitate before a flower.

    Escape through lightness.

  • Verith'el

    You will not give them a reason. I will.

    His answer is the title of the book.

In this world reality is a sentence that is still being spoken. The cosmos is a grammar called the Spiral of Being, grown from a tree named the Ashvattha, and power means reading deeper into the language everything is made of.

The book presents itself as a scripture, translated thousands of years after its events. Its history exists twice in my notes, once as it happened and once as later civilizations misremember it. The story bible is more than three hundred files. Most of them will never appear in the book, and the story stands on them anyway.

Fifteen books planned, about three million words. Nobody assigned any of it.
